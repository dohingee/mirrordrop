MirrorDrop shows your iPhone screen on a TV on the same Wi-Fi. Share trip photos, family videos and clips you found online with everyone on the big screen.

SIMPLE
(1) Choose your TV, (2) tap the big start button, then "Start Broadcast". Now open Photos, Safari or any app and it appears on the TV. First time? The built-in guide walks you through each step.

NO SIGN-UP, NO SUBSCRIPTION
- No account, no sign-in.
- Every feature is free: screen sending, sound, TV volume. No time limit, no watermark, no subscription.

STAYS IN YOUR HOME
- Your iPhone hands the video directly to the TV. Your screen and sound never go to the internet or any server, and nothing is recorded.

YOUR CHOICE OF SOUND
- Play the sound on the TV or on the phone, and switch at any time, even while sending.
- Change the TV volume or mute it from the app once connected.

TELLS YOU WHEN IT STOPS
- iOS ends screen sharing when the iPhone locks. Allow notifications and MirrorDrop tells you when sending ended; tap the alert to start again.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Works with TVs and streaming sticks that support Google Cast. The iPhone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi (TVs cannot be found on guest Wi-Fi or with AP/client isolation).
- The TV shows the screen about 4 seconds behind the iPhone, in 720p. Made for photos and videos, not for fast games.
- iOS sends copy-protected video (for example Netflix) as black. For those apps, use their own TV/Cast button.
- While sending, everything on the iPhone screen, including notifications, is visible on the TV.
- Requires iOS 17 or later.
- MirrorDrop is free and shows ads. No ads are shown while your screen is being sent to the TV.
