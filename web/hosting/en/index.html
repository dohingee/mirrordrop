MirrorDrop

A free app that shows your iPhone screen on a TV with Google Cast on the same Wi-Fi. Enjoy photos and videos together on the big screen.

How it works

  • (1) Pick your TV, (2) tap the big start button, then "Start Broadcast". Open any app and it appears on the TV.
  • Choose where the sound plays: TV or phone. Change the TV volume from the app.
  • No sign-up, no account, no subscription, no locked features.

Good to know

  • The TV shows the screen about 4 seconds behind the iPhone (720p). Not suitable for fast games.
  • The iPhone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi. TVs cannot be found on guest Wi-Fi or with AP/client isolation.
  • Copy-protected video (for example Netflix) appears black on the TV.
  • Sending ends when the iPhone locks.
  • Your screen goes to the TV only inside your home Wi-Fi. It never goes to the internet.

Privacy

Your screen and sound are never recorded or sent to the developer. The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads, never while your screen is being sent. See the Privacy Policy.
