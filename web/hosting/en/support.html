FAQ

The app cannot find my TV.

  • Make sure the iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi and the TV (or the stick plugged into it) is on.
  • Guest Wi-Fi or "AP isolation" (client isolation) on the router hides devices from each other.
  • Settings > MirrorDrop > Local Network must be on.
  • Only TVs and sticks with Google Cast are supported. AirPlay-only TVs are not.

The TV is behind my iPhone.

About 4 seconds is normal. The app is made for photos and videos, not fast games.

Netflix and similar videos are black.

iOS sends copy-protected video as black when the screen is shared. The app cannot change this. If that app has its own Cast button, use it instead.

Sending stops by itself.

iOS ends screen sharing when the iPhone locks. For long viewing set Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock to Never. Allow notifications to be told when sending stops.

Sound plays on both the phone and the TV.

Choose TV as the sound output and turn the iPhone volume all the way down to hear it only on the TV. Choose Phone to send the picture only.

I saw an inappropriate ad.

Tap "Report an ad" in the app, or email the time and what the ad showed to kimyeoware@gmail.com, and we will block it.

Where is my screen stored?

Nowhere. It goes only from the iPhone to the TV on the same Wi-Fi and is removed from memory after a few seconds. See the Privacy Policy.
